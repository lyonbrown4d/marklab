# Welcome to Marklab

This is your default workspace. Write in local Markdown files, connect your notes, and explore them on the map.

Hold Ctrl (Windows/Linux) or Command (macOS) and click the [Marklab User Guide](User%20Guide/00-Start-Here.md) to open it. Try a short exercise, then explore the other features.

These pages are ordinary notes. Edit, move, or delete them freely; deleted guide pages are not forcibly recreated.
