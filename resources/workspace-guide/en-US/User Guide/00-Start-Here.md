# Start Here

Marklab combines a Markdown editor, a file browser, and a map of linked notes. Notes stay in local files; sync and AI are optional.

## Around the workspace

The menu beside the workspace name opens or creates workspaces. The sidebar lists files, and opened notes appear in tabs. **Files** and **Map** switch between folders and relationships.

Quickly tap Shift twice to open the command palette for files, commands, and settings. In the editor, Ctrl-click (Windows/Linux) or Command-click (macOS) opens a note link.

> This guide is also a small linked workspace: each chapter is a note, and its links form the map.
